FingVibe 1.2 adds scan-evidence activity views for devices, networks and a person's assigned equipment, together with automatic usage categories and manual overrides.
It also brings checkpointed background discovery, read-only SQL reporting and Windows discovery notifications while keeping unknown observations distinct from offline devices or human presence.

## Major changes

- **Activity and history:** browse a device's 7-day, 30-day and weekday activity in 1-4-hour groups, with responding, nonresponding, unknown and partial evidence kept distinct. Network activity adds distinct-device counts, exact weekday averages and current room, device-type and usage-category filters; Person equipment reuses those grids and shows latest-scan equipment status, scan age and older-scan warnings, not whether a person is home. Completed-scan evidence, including empty scans and actual virtual associations, is retained without inventing missing older history. Delivered through [#252](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/252), retained implementation in [#272](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/272), Device Activity restoration [#280](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/280) with nested async-lifecycle work [#295](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/295), observation restoration [#296](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/296), Network [#304](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/304) and Person equipment [#315](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/315).
- **Usage categories:** automatic Permanent, Personal and Guest classification from recent selected-network scan evidence, with Unclassified and limited-evidence explanations, durable manual overrides, and a sortable/filterable Devices column. Assignments offers independent Save, Cancel and Automatic actions; this is not ownership or presence inference. [#255](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/255), presentation refinements [#309](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/309).
- **SQL reports:** a read-only Tools editor exposes documented reporting views over saved inventory, assignments, services and history, with examples, cancellation and explicit time/row/payload limits. The improved full-width results, shared Run/Cancel/status row and separate List of tables make schemas easier to explore without expanding write access. [#250](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/250), [#332](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/332).
- **Background scanning:** confirmed ping results are saved and browsable before fresh ARP discovery and two delayed ping retries finish; incomplete checkpoints survive restart without inventing completed history or new-device alerts. Background ARP now allows up to 32 simultaneous fresh requests while preserving adapter scoping and cancellation. The Devices Status filter is remembered across launches. [#220](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/220), [#308](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/308).
- **Discovery alerts:** a count-first popup offers full-row device actions and a grouped Windows notification for qualifying saved manual or scheduled scans, including while the app is visible. Activation reopens the saved discovery list without rescanning or notifying again; Windows notification settings can suppress presentation. [#317](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/317).

## Other user-facing changes

- **Navigation and layout:** Scan LAN is available beside the shared network selector on every page and subpage, retaining the current page, device/person/room context and drafts during scans. Device Details now separates Overview from History, with a saved-observation summary and View device history link; switching tabs preserves drafts and paging, while opening a device starts on Overview. [#321](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/321), [#310](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/310).
- **Devices presentation:** the final column keeps its separator; raw Private vendor values are shown as Unknown and locally administered results as Randomized MAC, without replacing raw evidence or named corrections. Eligible filters gain an exclusive Any-known-value choice; usage cells expose category source and provisional/unavailable explanations accessibly. [#309](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/309).
- **Help and installation:** a searchable, illustrated plain-English user manual covers installation, scans, data, tools and recovery, with a prominent README entry point. Version 1.2 retains the portable ZIP and unsigned per-user Setup installer; upgrade checks cover higher-build 1.0 and 1.1 installations, saved inventory/history/settings preservation and subsequent downgrade rejection. [#288](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/288), [#290](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/290), [#335](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/335).

## Engineering and contributor improvements

- **Native regression foundation:** shared bounded readiness, isolated fixtures, failure-preserving cleanup, phase/call diagnostics and observable async lifecycle tests improve coverage without weakening assertions. Independent Device Details, Devices, Sidebar and Tools groups retain coverage while reducing individual job runtimes; parallelism can increase total runner use and does not guarantee a faster whole pipeline. Includes [#277](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/277), [#280](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/280) and its nested [#294](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/294)/[#295](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/295), observation reachability [#296](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/296), tab-aware splitting [#310](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/310), Devices [#313](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/313), Sidebar/Tools and artifact-ID recovery [#316](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/316), and independent scan-presentation coverage [#321](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/321).
- **Validation and CI:** independent Baseline/Focused/Full execution reuses the native harness; Full validation runs on main pushes and remains manually dispatchable, while observational comparison is not a replacement merge gate. NuGet caching and independent native start reduce redundant waiting; maintained Node.js 24 Actions retain existing security/defaults, and authenticated, checksum-verified Gitleaks downloads retain fail-closed scanning. [#281](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/281), [#272](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/272), [#278](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/278), [#258](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/258).
- **Maintainability:** People/Rooms presentation and dialogs now belong to focused views/helpers while the shell retains navigation, serialized persistence and cross-page orchestration; existing editing, assignments and error behavior are preserved. [#291](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/291).
- **Development workflows, not application automation:** exploratory diagrams, local Dispatcher/bounded Developer instructions, host-tool inheritance, historical reconciliation and compact read-only preflight were added, followed by a three-repair policy and correlation fixes. Preflight repair-count validation now matches the approved 0-3 integer range without releasing retained claims or granting extra repairs. A separate two-developer synthetic experiment records feasibility, incidents and human recovery, not proof of unattended reliability or activation of production schedules. [#218](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/218), [#223](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/223), [#247](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/247), [#249](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/249), [#283](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/283), [#286](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/286), [#300](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/300), [#305](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/305), [#330](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/330), [#334](https://github.com/iricigor/fingvibe/pull/334).
