    FingVibe user manual¶

    Discover your local network. Understand and organize your devices.

    FingVibe is a Windows 11 application. It scans your local network, shows devices that respond, and helps you organize them by person and room.

    You do not need programming knowledge to use this manual. Start with Install and update, then follow Your first scan.

    What this manual describes¶

    This initial manual was checked against release v1.1.107 on 2026-11-09. It describes available behavior, not planned features. Your version is shown in Tools > About FingVibe.

    Activity grids and the Completed Scan Observations table are temporarily unavailable in this version. First/last-seen summaries, saved scan evidence, and usage categories remain available. See History.

    Select a screenshot to open it at full size. Screenshots may show a different test-build number. Their application source matches this release; sample values and test-only messages are not instructions.

    A scan is a sample, not continuous monitoring

    Online means a device responded in its latest applicable scan evidence. Offline does not prove a device is turned off. An assigned device does not prove its owner is present. Only scan networks you own or have permission to inspect.

    Find help¶

    Use the search box to find a term or task. On a small screen, open the menu, choose a section, then choose a chapter. Common problems gives practical checks before you report a bug.

    Downloads, source code, and issue reports are available in the FingVibe repository.
